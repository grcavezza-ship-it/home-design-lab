The Renovation Story

Born from the skeletal remains of a mid-century modernist retreat, Villa Orizzonte has been reimagined as a dialogue between architectural precision and the untamed botanical landscape of the Como shoreline. Our studio focused on "the horizon" as the primary design axis, ensuring every structural element frames the water without obstruction.

The restoration involved the delicate integration of sustainable geothermal systems and a bespoke automation layer that vanishes into the materiality of the walls. It is not just a residence, but a living blueprint of future-proof luxury.
