Case History // 042

Attico Prisma

A vertical sanctuary where light is the primary building material. A 400m² penthouse renovation redefining the intersection of transparency and privacy.

01. The Context

Balancing the
Uncontainable.

The original space was a fragmented series of dark chambers atop a historic limestone building. The challenge was twofold: to maximize the panoramic views of the city's green belt without compromising the thermal integrity of the home, and to create a botanical interior that could thrive in high-altitude direct sunlight.

Structural limitations meant we couldn't add weight, requiring a surgical approach to material selection—prioritizing lightness and translucency over traditional masonry.
